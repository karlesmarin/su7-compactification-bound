  So the residual is NOT a function of the parity-blind moments.  Whatever it is, it reads
  the parity SPLIT -- and that is the localisation, established by one comparison instead of
  by an ordering over a collinear family.  The ordering test above was the wrong instrument,
  not a negative result.

  AND THE SPLIT IS A NEAR-CANCELLATION, which is why a small error there is a large error
  here.  D = A_2 - (3/4) B_2 on these two rows:

  row            A_2    (3/4) B_2 D = difference      D / A_2
  (1)         78.000       76.125         1.8750        2.40 %
  (2)         79.000       75.375         3.6250        4.59 %

  D is a two-percent residue of A_2, so the one-percent difference in the split between these
  two rows moves D by a factor of 1.93.  And x ~ sqrt(D), so it moves alpha_min by 39 % --
  which is the size of the residual itself.  A small error in the antiperiodic trace would
  therefore reproduce this, and that is a testable repair rather than a hope:

  row   lambda needed    alpha at it         theirs
  (1)         1.0077        0.04300        0.04300
  (2)         1.0020        0.08100        0.08100
  (3)         1.0140        0.02100        0.02100
  (4)         1.0127        0.02600        0.02600
  (5)         1.0080        0.04300        0.04300

  and the quantity to judge is lambda MINUS ONE, not lambda.  With lambda near unity a
  spread of 0.012 looks tight and is not: the perturbation itself is what varies.

  row       lambda - 1
  (1)           0.0077
  (2)           0.0020
  (3)           0.0140
  (4)           0.0127
  (5)           0.0080

  lambda      : 1.0020 to 1.0140, a spread of 0.0120
  lambda - 1  : 0.0020 to 0.0140, a ratio of 7.1 between the largest and the smallest

  SO IT IS NOT A COMMON RESCALING.  The required perturbation varies by a factor of 7
  across the five rows, so a single factor on the antiperiodic sector does not fix them,
  and this joins the one-parameter repairs anchor_trend.py already excluded.

  BUT WHAT IT DOES ESTABLISH IS THE SIZE, AND THAT IS THE USEFUL PART.  Every row needs a
  perturbation of the SAME SIGN and of order ONE PERCENT -- 0.2 % to 1.4 % -- to land on
  their published alpha_min.  A residual that presents as a factor of two in alpha is
  therefore consistent with a sub-percent discrepancy in one trace, amplified by the
  near-cancellation.  That changes what the caveat means: not 'our potential is wrong by
  a factor of two' but 'one trace may be wrong in its second digit, where D has no room'.

3 -- WHAT WOULD SETTLE IT, STATED AS A REQUIREMENT AND NOT AS A HOPE
====================================================================================================
  The deduction is: the machinery is validated at the vGIQ point and fails at \cite{KM25}'s,
  so the residual lives in what differs between them.  To turn that into a measurement one
  needs rows that move Delta WITHOUT moving D, and the five published ones do not:

  quantity                              min          max
  D                                  1.1250       3.6250
  A_4                              189.0000     271.0000
  Delta_1                          -23.5000     -17.5000
  ratio                              1.0318       2.0772

  The ratio spans 1.03 to 2.08 -- a factor of 2.01 -- across a family whose three candidate
  explanatory variables are mutually collinear.  So the requirement is not another statistic
  on these five points.  It is ONE published potential, by anyone, at two parities, against
  which our F can be evaluated -- the anchor for Delta != 0 that section open already names as
  the sharpest thing missing.  What this file adds is WHY it is the sharpest: not because the
  gap is unexplained, but because the working anchor is blind exactly where the gap lives, so
  no amount of re-reading the vGIQ agreement can ever bound it.

  (i) THE TEST MUST BE ABLE TO SAY NO.  A variable that cannot order the ratios has to come
      back non-monotone; shuffle the ratios against D and count how many of the 120
      re-pairings survive, which is anchor_trend.py's own control:
      re-pairings of the five ratios to the five D that are monotone : 2 of 120

  (ii) AND THE ROWS MUST BE THE PUBLISHED ONES.  ratio = our alpha_min over theirs.  The
       paper's 1.94 and 1.20 are the MEANS OF THE TWO GROUPS, not the extremes -- I compared
       them against the extremes first and read a discrepancy that was not there.  It has to
       be the means that come back, or this file is not reading the paper's five rows:
       mean with a 48    : 1.941 over 2 rows   (paper: 1.94)
       mean without a 48 : 1.203 over 3 rows   (paper: 1.20)
       the extremes, for contrast : 2.08 and 1.03 -- neither is what the paper quotes

  (iv) AND THE PARITY LABEL p IS NOT THE PERIODICITY s.  This control exists because I tried to
