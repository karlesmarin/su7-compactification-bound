2 -- THE ODD INDEX IS NOT.  the tell is what the value at x=0 IS
====================================================================================================
At x = 0 the series is zeta(p).  For even p that is a rational multiple of pi^p -- so the whole
function can be, and is, a polynomial with rational coefficients times a power of pi.  For odd p
no such expression is known, and zeta(3) and zeta(5) are not rational multiples of any power of
pi (zeta(3) is known irrational; no polynomial identity exists for either).

  The even rows are rational multiples of pi^p -- the Bernoulli numbers.  The odd rows are not,
  to the precision of an integer-relation search.  That is the whole difference.
