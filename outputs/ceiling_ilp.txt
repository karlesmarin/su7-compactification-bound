2 -- THE MOD-6 LAW:   8D = 2 A4 + 3   (mod 6)   for every bulk content
====================================================================================================
Proof.  Per term (m, s, c) of the potential, with c and m integers for matter:
   A4 gets  m c^4 (s=+1 only);   8D = 8A2 - 6B2  gets  8 m c^2 (s=+1) or -6 m c^2 (s=-1).
   mod 3:  s=-1 gives -6mc^2 = 0;  s=+1 gives  m c^2 (8 + c^2) = m c^2 (c^2 + 2) = 0,
           because c^4 = c^2 (mod 3) for EVERY integer c (Fermat).  So 8D + A4 = 0 (mod 3).
   mod 2:  matter contributes 8A2 - 6B2, even; the gauge sector contributes -27, odd.
   Together:  8D + A4 = 0 (mod 3)  and  8D odd  =>  8D = 2A4 + 3 (mod 6).
