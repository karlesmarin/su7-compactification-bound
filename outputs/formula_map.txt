   per-term S_k = m c^{2k},  Delta_k = m c^{2k} s,  and 2^p = 32/4^k since p = 5-2k

   VERDICT: the geometric form IS the per-term form, identically in k, up to the normalisation 2^p

================================================================================================
WHAT THE GEOMETRIC FORM SAYS, once it is trusted
================================================================================================
   a PERIODIC term contributes one mode, sigma = c^2/4;
   an ANTIPERIODIC one contributes two, sigma = c^2/4 and sigma = c^2, with opposite signs.
   The ladder is therefore a sum of geometric sequences in k, and its fixed point is sigma = 1:

      c = 1 :  sigma = c^2/4 = 1/4      c^2 = 1     decays by 1/4 per rung
      c = 2 :  sigma = c^2/4 = 1        c^2 = 4     FIXED POINT: same weight at every rung
      c = 3 :  sigma = c^2/4 = 9/4      c^2 = 9     grows

   So CHARGE TWO is the pivot of the whole ladder, and it is a statement about the
   representation content, not about the loop integrals.

================================================================================================
THE THREE RUNGS, AND WHY THE LAST ONE IS DIFFERENT
================================================================================================
     k    p        2^p                   what it is
     0    5         32               the value F(0)
     1    3          8           the curvature -> D
     2    1          2            the fourth moment

   The weight of Delta in rung k is (2^p - 1)/2^p = 1 - 2^-p: 31/32, 7/8, 1/2.
   At p = 1 the two halves of (1) diverge separately and only their difference survives --
   that is the logarithm, and it is why there is no polynomial.

THE JOIN: where each loop order reads the group
================================================================================================
   C2 = sum_i H_i^2 + sum_alpha E^alpha E^-alpha    -- Cartan part + root part

   ONE loop  reads  q_w = <w,T>                 the CARTAN part: a weight's own charge
   TWO loops read   |<w+alpha|E^alpha|w>|^2     the ROOT part: how two weights are joined

   and the sum rule (4) says the root part, resolved by charge, is fixed by C2 and |w|^2:
       sum_Delta N_Delta(q) = sum_{w: q_w=q} mult(w) [C2 - |w|^2].

   That is the same split Guo-Du find by computing in four dimensions: B_4(C_b) at one loop,
   the individual angles, and B_4(C_bd) at two, the differences. An angle is a weight; a
   difference of angles is a root. The two derivations meet.

================================================================================================
THE Z2 THAT APPEARS UNDER FOUR NAMES
================================================================================================
   the ladder       s = +-1 in sum_n s^n/n^p       even vs odd winding
   the moments      S vs Delta                     parity-blind vs parity-sensitive
   the labels       eta * eta'                     the two orbifold parities
   the SU(4) model  A vs B                         identity vs coset multiplicity

   They are one Z2. The criterion measured today closes the circle: the alpha_2 fold is legal
   exactly when A = B on every half-integer charge -- that is, exactly when Delta receives
   nothing from them, which is exactly when the parity-sensitive half of (1) is silent.
