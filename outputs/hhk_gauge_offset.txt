3 -- THEIR (4.5) + (4.6), SUMMED, AGAINST THE FIRST LINE OF (4.7)
====================================================================================================
  (4.5), gauginos:
     -4[ N^(++)(e0+v(b)) + N^(--)(e0+v(-b)) + N^(+-)(e0+v(b+1/2)) + N^(-+)(e0+v(-b-1/2)) ]
  (4.6), gauge + ghost + sigma:
     (2N^(++)+2N^(--))(e0+de) + (2N^(--)+2N^(++))(e0-de)
       + (2N^(+-)+2N^(-+))(e0+v(1/2)) + (2N^(-+)+2N^(+-))(e0+v(1/2))

  v is even and w(b) = v(1/2) + v(b) - v(b+1/2), so with S = N^(+-)+N^(-+) and
  N^(++)+N^(--) = N^2-1-S the v-dependent part of the sum is

     -4(N^2-1-S) v(b)  -  4 S v(b+1/2)  +  4 S v(1/2)
        = -4(N^2-1) v(b)  +  4 S [ v(b) - v(b+1/2) + v(1/2) ]
        = -4(N^2-1) v(b)  +  4 S w(b) .

  which is their (4.7) FIRST line, exactly:  -4(N^2-1)v + 4(N_Ad^(+-) + N_Ad^(-+)) w.
  The Delta-epsilon terms cancel between the two lines of (4.6), as they say.

4 -- VERDICT, WITH THEIR OWN (4.13) AS ARBITER
====================================================================================================
  (4.7) line 1 : -4(N^2-1)v + 4 S w        with S = 2(p+s)(q+r)  ->  + 8(p+s)(q+r) w
  (4.7) line 2 : -4(N^2-1)v + 4 (p+s)(q+r) w                     ->  half of line 1

  (4.13) collects the w-bracket as  4 { (p+s)(q+r)(2 - 2n_Ad^(+) + ...) + ... } w, so with no
  matter at all its gauge part is  4 * (p+s)(q+r) * 2 * w = 8 (p+s)(q+r) w.

  Two of their equations give 8 and one gives 4, and the two are the ones everything else
  rests on.  So the dropped factor is in the SECOND line of (4.7), it is a slip in the
  substitution and not in the physics, and nothing downstream is affected: the +2 offset in
  their a is the correct one.

  WHAT IT MEANS FOR US.  Their gauge sector contributes the EVEN INTEGER +2 to a and NOTHING
  to b.  Both functionals can therefore vanish, and they treat those cases explicitly --
  their section (i) is 'The case with a = 0', and their (4.15) lists
  'n_F^(+) = n_F^(-) => completely degenerate'.  In the model of this paper the gauge sector
  contributes -7/2, a half-integer, so 8D, 2U and 2W are odd for every content and the
  degenerate case they enumerate cannot be reached.  Same architecture, opposite outcome,
  and the difference is one number.
