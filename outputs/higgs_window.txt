1 -- THE CEILING RISES WITH m_h, SO THE TOP OF THE WINDOW IS THE CONSERVATIVE CHOICE
====================================================================================================
  mu ~ m_h^2, and (II) is x^2 = 12 zeta(3) D / (A_4 + 6 mu), so a larger m_h means a larger
  denominator, a smaller x, and 1/R5 = 2 pi m_W / x LARGER.  Measured rather than asserted,
  at the fixed vertex A_4 = 104 so that only m_h moves:

         m_h           mu      1/R5 at 104
      124.00      78.8658           9038.3
      125.00      80.1429           9098.1
      125.20      80.3996           9110.1
      126.00      81.4304           9158.0
      127.00      82.7280           9218.0
      128.00      84.0360           9278.0

  CONTROL -- strictly increasing in m_h : True
  So quoting the ceiling at 127 is right for an upper bound over the window.  What follows is
  not a correction to that; it is the other number, which the paper does not give.

3 -- THE TWO NUMBERS, AND WHAT EACH ONE MEANS
====================================================================================================
  Both are true and they quantify different statements:

     1/R5 <= 9.22 TeV   for any bulk content whose Higgs mass lies ANYWHERE in [125, 127]
     1/R5 <= 9.09 TeV   for any bulk content whose Higgs mass is the MEASURED one

  a 1.4 % difference, and the second is the one a reader wanting a number about the world
  should take.
