so.  An ANTIPERIODIC term carries TWO modes, not one:

      s = +1 :  one mode,  sigma = c^2/4

antiperiodic ones.  Three rungs and two modes leave exactly one linear relation; a third mode
destroys it.  The gauge sector reaches only {1/4, 1} -- which is WHY section 3 passes.
