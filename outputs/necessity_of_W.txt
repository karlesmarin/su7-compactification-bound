1 -- THE DEPTH OF THE ELECTROWEAK WELL, IN CLOSED FORM
====================================================================================================
  F(x) - F(0) = -zeta(3) D x^2/2 + x^4 (G - A_4 ln x)/24 + R.
  Stationarity (eq. amin) gives  G - A_4 ln x = [24 zeta(3) D / x^2 + A_4]/4, so the quartic
  term becomes  zeta(3) D x^2/4 + A_4 x^4/96  and the depth collapses to

      F(x*) - F(0) = - zeta(3) D x*^2/4 + A_4 x*^4/96 .

  Then (II), which holds once m_h is pinned, gives 6 mu + A_4 = 12 zeta(3) D / x*^2, hence
  A_4 = 12 zeta(3) D/x*^2 - 6 mu, and substituting:

      F(x*) - F(0) = - zeta(3) D x*^2 / 8  -  mu x*^4 / 16 .
