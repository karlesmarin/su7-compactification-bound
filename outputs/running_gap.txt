                                                       ours       x 2.08

ceiling for contents that can afford the escape       3.97 T       8.24 T

  EXTRAPOLATIONS, NOT BOUNDS.  A ratio measured on five rows is a measurement of a
  discrepancy, not a weight, and a two-loop term is under no obligation to be a uniform
  rescaling of anything.  The paper marks them with a dagger for that reason.
