  says 10034, and calls the difference 'the gap between a relaxation and its lattice'.  But
  the enumeration used numeric_min, which is the GLOBAL minimiser, so it was screening false
  vacua all along without saying so.  The certificate was not.  If that is the whole story,
  the enumeration's champions must all have W > 0 and the constrained ceiling must land on
  top of them.
